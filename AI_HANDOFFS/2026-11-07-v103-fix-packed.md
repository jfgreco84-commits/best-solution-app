## WHAT CHANGED

**✏️ Fix Packed** button on the show's 📦 Show stock card (running shows only). One box per size,
filled with what the app says was packed. Type the real number, Save.

- **Packed** moves by the difference. Stock brought from the garage later stays separate.
- **Garage** box (checked by default): the extra comes off the garage, a shortfall goes back. Never below 0;
  a shortfall is named in the toast. Unchecked: garage not touched.
- **Opening count** moves too, but only where it still equals the old packed number. A count typed by hand
  is kept, and extra already entered as restock on the opening day stays restock. So sold and on hand
  never change by themselves.
- Refused on a finalized day. Logged in the show's log, the audit log and Garage History.
- Model: `fixPackedCounts(sh,want,fromGarage,st,why)`; UI: `openFixPacked`, `applyFixPackedCounts`.
