## Froggy's rule

- **Isaiah** is paid **40% of his own sales**, every show.
- **Everyone else** gets **35%**.
- On any day someone works **side by side with the owner**, the booth's 35% is split with him. The owner's share is kept, and that holds for Isaiah too.
- **Pay already paid stays as it is.** Pay typed in by hand is accurate.

## How the app does it

- `rep.commRate` holds each rep's rate (a fraction); `repCommRate(id)` returns 0.35 when it isn't set. Migration `pay_rules_v80` sets Isaiah to 0.40 once. Edit Rep has a "Commission (% of their own sales)" field.
- `PAY_RULES_FROM = '2026-10-17'` sits at the top of the file. Shows dated on or after it (Fox & Found Oct onward) get the new rules in `commissionDay`:
  - Owner on the booth that day (`dayBoothCrew`), or on a show with no booths and `repOnlyShow` false: 35% split n ways, owner's share kept.
  - Otherwise each person is paid at their own rate, on their own drawer when it is split by seller, or on an even share of the booth.
  - A show with no booths uses `tableCrew(sh)` (its reps plus the owner unless it is rep-only).
- `payRulesOn(sh)` (new-rule show, not `payFromCommission`, not passed) makes `commissionSyncPay` fill `day.repPay` from the rule on every save. It only writes days with sales.
- **Hand pay wins.**
  - Typing a different number in Day Pay (per day or per rep) sets `sh.repPayManual[repId]=true`, and that rep's pay on that show is never touched again.
  - Clearing every day for that rep hands them back to automatic.
  - Pay already on a show is marked as typed the first time `commissionSyncPay` sees it under the rules (`sh.payRulesSeen`, set only when there was pay to keep or the sync wrote pay). That covers the v80 load, a show whose date is moved past Oct 17, and a show arriving from an older device.
- Shows before Oct 17 are untouched: no rate change, and `commissionDay` uses the old flat show rate there (Cranberry's `payFromCommission` sync stays at 35%).
- A later migration that writes `repPay` on an Oct 17+ show that already has `payRulesSeen` must also set `repPayManual` for those reps, or the sync will replace it.
- v81 adds a line under each rep on the day screen saying why the pay is what it is ("40% of $300", "35% of $400 split 2 ways with you", "typed by you"), plus a crew note on shows with no booths so a forgotten Rep-only tick is visible.
