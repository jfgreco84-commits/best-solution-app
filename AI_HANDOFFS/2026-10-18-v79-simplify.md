Froggy approved items 1 to 7 of the simplification list. Items 8 to 10 (retire old migrations, merge the two profit engines, retire the package loader) are code cleanup for v83 (v80 became the pay rules, v81 the show-day safety pass, v82 paid-ahead deposits).

## What changed for the owner

1. **One profit number.** Home's "NET PROFIT (YEAR)" and "YOU KEEP" now come straight from `calcYTD()`, the same number the P&L tab shows. The old "clean keep" from completed shows only is gone. `homeCtx().yearNet` returns the same number.
2. **One place for gas.** The Day Expenses screen has no gas box. Gas is set once per show in ✏️ Edit Show (miles, or Actual Gas $). Fuel is gone from the Other Expenses picker. Old day gas and old fuel receipts still count: `showGasLogged(sh)` adds them, `saveExp` keeps a day's old `gas`, and the show screen shows one gas line.
3. **Setup cleanup.** Order is now Cloud, then one 💾 Backup & Restore card, then rates, costs, prices and reports. The Sales Reps card left Setup (the REPS tab owns it). The package loader, the Phase 2b box, device backups, emergency restore, raw export/import and the Danger Zone sit in a collapsed "🛠️ Advanced (only when Claude asks)" section.
4. **6 tabs.** Home, Stock, Shows, Reps, P&L, Setup. Calendar is a toggle at the top of Shows, and Supplies is a toggle at the top of Stock (`viewToggleHTML`). `go('calendar')` and `go('supplies')` still work and light up the parent tab. The supply low-stock badge sits on Stock.
5. **Payout Sheet only on Teams.** The Money tab shows a button that jumps to it.
6. **Paste Claude Update removed** (the card and `applyClaudeCmd`).
7. **Online sales in the year.** `calcYTD` adds online amount to gross, cost to COGS and postage to expenses, and returns `online`. The P&L has an "Online sales postage" line, and Gross Revenue notes "incl. $X online". The accountant package and the season CSV carry an online row, so the CSV rows still sum to the season total. calcYTD gas now also counts day gas and fuel receipts.
