### 5. Your data and how it stays safe

- **Everything saves on your phone and syncs to the cloud** (Supabase) when you are signed in. All 3 devices share the same data.
- **The newest copy wins.** If two devices change things at the same time without syncing, one overwrites the other. **Let a device sync before switching to another.**
- **When I (Claude) change your data, I send an update, not a request to type.** You open the new link and the app applies the change once. If you already typed it yourself, it skips it so nothing gets doubled.
- **Backups:** SETUP → download a backup file any time. Keep one before big changes.

## PART 2: FOR THE NEXT BUILDER (developer detail)

### 6. How the app is built

- **One file:** `BEST_SOLUTION_APP.html`, about 12,000 lines of vanilla JS in one inline script. Supabase JS comes from a CDN.
- **Other files:**
  - `index.html` redirects to the app.
  - `BEST_SOLUTION_APP_BLANK.html` is now just an old-copy redirect.
  - `planner.html` is a static printable planner and is not wired to data.
  - `packages/` holds approved JSON data packages.
  - `AI_HANDOFFS/` holds per-version notes; `LATEST.md` is the index.
- **State:** a single object `S`, saved to localStorage key `dd_bs_v7`.
  - `saveS()` stamps `_updatedAt`, runs `auditSyncReturns()` and `commissionSyncAll()`, writes, re-renders, and queues a cloud push (800 ms debounce).
- **Boot:** `loadS()` → `applyOneTimeUpdates(d)` (migrations) → `commissionSyncAll()` → `go('home')` → `cloudInit()`. A boot error prints on screen.
- **Top-level state keys (tests enforce this set, so do not add new ones):** `v, reps, costs, shows, prices, freight, _applied, settings, supplies, inventory, transfers, _updatedAt, productDebt, batmanBorrow, otherExpenses, cogsHistory, cogsHistoryMeta`. New features store inside `settings`; online sales use `S.settings.onlineSales`.
- **Cloud:**
  - Supabase table `app_data`, row `data_key='bs_state'`: the whole document, protected by RLS on `user_id`.
  - Whole-document last writer wins; this is not a record merge.
  - `cloudHydrate` compares `_updatedAt` and pauses behind a safety prompt if the cloud has more completed shows.
  - `adoptCloud` backs up locally first (keeps 3 `dd_bs_backup_*`).
  - Phase 2B "Cloud Reconciliation" is the only additive merge.
- **Replay guard:** `mrg*`, with a ledger in `dd_bs_seedlog_v1`. Any migration that creates a show must go through `mrgPushShow` and be listed in `MRG_SEED_RECORDS`.

### 7. Products and money rules

**SKUs** (key, short name, default price, cost in the Aug 2026+ period):

| Key | Short | Price | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| `32oz` | 32oz | $25 | $4.50 |
| `16oz` | 16oz | $20 | $3.75 |
| `8oz` | 8oz | $15 | $3.25 |
| `2oz` | 2oz | $8 | $2.00 |
| `c5s` | C5-S | $10 | $2.80 |
| `c5l` | C5-L | $20 | $5.84 |

- **Naming:** Froggy says "C5" for small and "C5x" or "large" for C5-L.
- **COGS** is priced on the sale date: `calcCOGSOn` → `getCostOn`. Precedence: show override → `S.cogsHistory` period → `S.costs` → SKU default.
- **Prices:** global `S.prices` (read with `getP`); `sh.prices` is frozen at pack time. Read a show's price with `_psPrice(sh,k)`.
- **`calcShow(sh)`:** gross is money actually collected, not units × price.
  - Profit = gross − salesTax − COGS − expenses.
  - Expenses = booth (`max(boothCost, boothPaid)`) + day expenses + gas + `showExpenses` + `lodging` + supplies + rep day pay + worker payouts + card fees.
  - Card fees = (Square + Debit) × `sqFee` (3%).
  - Sales tax is backed out of card sales at `cardTax` (5%).
  - Tax reserve = profit × 28%.
- **Gas:** the auto estimate (`showGas`) is used only when no gas and no Fuel expense is logged. `showMiles` honours `sh.totalMiles` (v55) for IRS miles.

### 8. Booth shows, the truck, and the audit

- A booth show has `sh.booths`. Per day, `d.boothCounts[boothId]` holds `morningCount`, `eveningCount`, `restock`, `lost`, `payments` and `sellerPayments[repId]`.
  - Other day fields: `d.boothCrew[boothId]` (crew), `d.garageIn` (a delivery counts as opening stock), and `d.truckCount` (a hand count).
- **`rollupDay(sh,di)`** is the only place a multi-booth day's totals are written. The day evening total posts only when every booth has closed.
- **`showLedger(sh)`:** per day, start / table / truck / left / end. The rule is: **everything at a show is on a table or in the truck.**
  - `truckShowOnHand` is everything still at the show.
  - `truckCountDiff` compares a hand count with the expected truck.
  - `fixPackedFromTruckCount` (v44) corrects the packed count without touching the garage.
- **`auditAll`** codes: `garageNeg`, `unpacked`, `overTable`, `noClose`, `noMorning`, `moneyNoCount`, `truckCount`, `boothOver`, `negSold`, `carry` (single table only), `returnDrift`, `staleActive`.
- **End show:** `showEndModal` → `confirmEndShow`. Returns = max(last evening count, packed − sold − lost). Sets `returned`, `completedAt`, `status='completed'`.

### 9. Pay system (v53 to v62)

- **Commission, `commissionDay(sh,di)`** at `showCommRate(sh)` (default 0.35), per booth:
  - If the owner is not on the crew and the drawer is split by seller, each seller gets rate × their own sales.
  - Otherwise the booth's commission is split evenly across the crew; the owner's share is kept, not paid.
- **`sh.payFromCommission`:** `commissionSyncPay` rewrites `day.repPay` to the exact commission on every save and on boot, so the P&L, rep pages, Reps & Workers and the payout sheet always agree. Manual Day Pay is blocked on these shows.
- **`sh.payRound = {repId: ±$}`:** rounding the owner chose.
  - Round-ups go on the last day with sales.
  - Round-downs come off the last day, then earlier days.
  - Rep "earned" and P&L labor therefore equal what was actually paid.
- **Extras:** a `sh.workers` entry with `repId` (Dave's $200 cooking). It counts in worker pay; `w.paid` tracks it.
- **Payout sheet:** `payoutSheetData(sh)` / `payoutSheetHTML(sh)`, booth shows only.
  - Shows each person's pay per day, commission, rounding, extra, total, paid, owed or over, sales credited, and booth worked.
  - **Paid ✓** (`payoutMarkPaid`) fills the extra first, then logs `rep.payments` with `showId`.
