# v82 paid-ahead deposits: the season number was too low (Oct 18 2026)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Branch** | `claude/prepaid-deposits-v82` |
| **App version** | v81 → **v82** |
| **Tests** | `node tests/paid-ahead-v82.test.js` (11 checks) |

Froggy saw $3,483.71 "yours to keep" on Home and said it was way too low.

## What was wrong

1. **Deposits on shows still to come were charged to this season's profit.** That was $3,070.90 on the Oct 17 board, across 15 shows:
   - Chicago Women's Expo $1,095
   - Wonderful World of Weddings (Jan 2027) $694.90
   - Whole Bead Show $195
   - Ten Hotel Bridal Expo (Jan 2027) $178
   - Galentine's (Feb 2027) $118
   - 10 more Oct 23 to Dec 12 shows, $60 to $103 each

   `calcShow` already charges a show's booth when the show is worked, so the deposit was hitting profit before the show had earned anything.
2. **The Home bar compared different things.** It put 2026 after the 28% tax reserve against 2025 net profit (`PREV_YEAR.net` $9,088), which is before tax.

## The fix

- `isPaidAhead(sh)` covers a show that is not completed, active, passed or missed, and is dated today or later (or has no date). `paidAheadTotal()` sums what has been paid on those shows.
- **`calcYTD` leaves paid-ahead money out of expenses** and returns `paidAhead`. Money paid on missed or passed shows stays in profit, because that money is gone.
- **P&L** keeps its lines reconciled and adds an info line: "Paid ahead for upcoming shows: $X. Not in profit yet". It also notes that deposits paid this year for next year's shows usually deduct in the year paid.
- **Season CSV and print report:** the old "Future shows" row is now "Missed / passed shows: money already spent". Paid-ahead money gets its own row at 0.00 profit, so the rows still sum to the total.
- **Accountant package:** adds a "PAID AHEAD FOR UPCOMING SHOWS" list with show, date and amount paid.
- **Home:**
  - The bar reads "Net $X = N% of 2025 net".
  - The overhead tile now covers purchases plus money lost on missed shows, and shows the paid-ahead total separately.
