## WHAT WAS FIXED

**Stock**

1. A minus sign typed in Pack for Show added that many to the garage. Now it is 0.
2. Pack for Show on a show that already had stock moved in from another show wiped that stock out. It now adds to it.
3. Starting a show on day 2 (rained out day 1) put the opening on day 1, so sales and COGS were lost and the close sent too much home. The opening now lands on today's day.
4. Leftovers moved to another show after the night count were returned to the garage by the first show as well, so units were counted twice. `endShowReturns` now subtracts show-to-show moves made on or after the last counted night.
5. A garage delivery (or a Batman return) bigger than the garage count stops the garage at 0, but its undo gave back the full amount. Records now carry `took` and undo gives back only that.
6. Undo Start left the day's restock, lost and deliveries behind. They are cleared now.
7. Small Sets Break apart gave back add-ons the build never took. Supplies now carry `inSets`.
8. Restoring a pre-v95 backup had no SET cost, so the whole cost schedule was refused until a reload. The SET blanks are now filled on restore.

**Money**

9. The Payout Sheet ignored pay typed by hand (v80 "hand pay wins"), so Paid ✓ logged the wrong amount. It now uses the typed pay.
10. **Year rollover (Jan 1 2027).** Year totals added up every year together. `calcYTD(Y)`, `yrShows`, `yrOther` and `olTotals(Y)` now count:
    - shows by the year they start
    - purchases, online sales and trips by their own date
    - anything with no date in this year

    Home is always this year. The P&L has a year switch when there is more than one year. The Accountant Package, mileage log, season CSV, expenses CSV and full report follow the P&L's year, so in January he can pull 2026 for the accountant. "2026" labels now come from the date.
11. The Full Export summary added in planned and missed shows and left out purchases and online sales. It now uses the same rules as the P&L, across all years.
12. The gas column on the Mileage screen showed the estimate while the total showed booked gas. Each row now shows booked gas (estimate + receipts), so the rows add up.
13. The Close Show popup left out sales tax, so its lines did not add up.
14. Average show gross no longer includes online sales.
15. A 0% tax setting stays 0 instead of falling back to 28%.
16. Lodging can no longer be saved as a negative.

**Shows**

17. Shrinking a show's days dropped days that had expenses, rep pay, notes or deliveries but no counts. Those now count as data.
18. A show with no date went to Missed the day after it was created. It now stays upcoming.
19. A booth with only a garage delivery could be removed, which wiped the delivery. Deliveries now count as data.
20. Calendar export:
    - Shows were timed 8am to 8am the day after, so a Sat-Sun show showed on Monday. They are now all-day events.
    - Commas, semicolons and line breaks are escaped.
    - The file name follows the year.

**Sync and backups**

21. `SYNC_PROTOCOL` 2 → 3. An older cached app (v94, no SET size) on a second device could load the cloud copy and save it back without the SET counts. It now refuses a protocol-3 copy and says to reload. **Raise it again with every new size or field an older app would drop.**
22. Two saves less than a second apart on slow signal sent two writes against the same cloud version. The second was refused as "another device saved first" and sync stopped. `cloudPush` now runs one at a time and a waiting push sends the newest copy once.
23. When a write landed but its answer was lost, the next read raised "Sync needs you". The pending write is now remembered (`_syncBase.pending`) and recognized as this device's own.
24. The daily auto copy now runs before the day's first save.
25. `backupLocal` keeps the newest 3 plain copies by time. The old text sort deleted the copy it had just made once 3 tagged copies existed.

## LEFT AS IS (tell Froggy if it matters)

- A show still marked planned on the day it runs is neither missed nor paid ahead. The auditor bar already flags a show that should have started.
- `garageCountOct22` has no `set` in its target. It only runs on a copy without the v89/v90 markers, and `small_set_v95` sets `set` to 0 right after, so the only effect is an extra empty Garage History line.
- File restore and paste import still do not reload. Device Backups and emergency restore do.
- `planner.html` is a static 2026 document.
